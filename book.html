02 · Before you ask

Booking FAQ

How far in advance should we book?

At least [X] weeks for most shows. For festivals and fest season (Dec to Mar), reach out earlier. Dates go fast.

Do you perform outside Mumbai?

Yes. For outstation shows, travel and stay for the crew are covered by the organiser on top of the fee.

Can we request specific songs?

Absolutely. Tell us in the form: Usool, Bohot Sara Cheeze, Murder of a Free Bird, I Wish, or Ehsaas for a slower moment. We'll build the set around it.

Can we book just one artist?

Yes. Pick Fentos, KANiF or the full crew in the form. If you're not sure who fits your crowd, choose "Not sure" and we'll suggest.

What does a show cost?

It depends on format, city, set length and crowd size. Send the form with a budget range and we'll reply with a clear quote. No surprises.

Can you make a custom song for our event or brand?

Yes. Anthems, jingles and original songs are part of our studio work. Pick "Studio / collab" above, or see services.
