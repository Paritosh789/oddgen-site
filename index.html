01 — About ODD GEN

A generation that doesn't fit the mould — so we built our own.

ODD GEN is a house of affiliated hip hop artists representing Mumbai. We do the whole thing under one roof — beats, bars, hooks, vocals, mix. Every track starts in our room and leaves finished.

Sounds rooted in our past culture, seen through a future generation's ears. We dropped our first music video, Murder of a Free Bird, in October 2022 — new artists with an old school soul.

Rap is the A-side. Now and then we flip the record for a love song — our B-side, made with the same care.
